## How reliable is this?

* Notes marked **(source)** come from public pages that were fetched in November 2026 (links at the bottom of each file).
* Several fan wikis (the Emergency fandom wiki) refused automated access, so details of the LA mod beyond the public summary come from the mod's published description and from general knowledge of the series. Those parts are marked **(general knowledge)** and should be verified against the mod's own documentation before anyone relies on them.
* Nothing here is copied from the games. OpenRescue RTS uses its own code, content and assets.
