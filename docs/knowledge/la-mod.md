# The Los Angeles mod

## What it is **(source)**

A community modification for Emergency 4 (v2.1) and Emergency 3 by "Hoppah" that replaces the original units and personnel with ones found in Los Angeles. Public summary: 10 new missions, an optional free-play map, 50+ playable units, new sirens, directional lights and flood lights, a fire station, a police station and a hospital, automatic weapons and ballistic shields, bomb robots and motorcycles.

## What it teaches us **(source + general knowledge)**

* **Local flavour makes a city feel real.** Vehicles, uniforms, sirens and building names belong to one place. We do the same for the Netherlands: P2000 pager lines, Dutch street names and addresses, Dutch vehicle names and codes (TS, HW, HV, AMB, OvD, CoPI, LGH, MMT).
* **Many specialised units, each with one clear job.** The mod's strength is the breadth of the fleet: SWAT/ballistic units, bomb robot, motorcycles, command vehicles. We carry: bomb squad with robot, riot van (ME), motorcycle, hazmat unit, water tanker, command vehicles.
* **Sirens and lights are a signal.** Lights and sirens belong to urgent runs only; returning units drive quietly. We do: prio 1 = lights and siren, prio 2 = lights only, return trips silent.
* **Armed police.** Pistols and ballistic shields give arrests weight. We do: officers shout, draw the pistol, suspects either surrender or run.
* **Crowd control scenarios** (demonstrations, riots) are separate missions with ME units. We do: demonstration and city-riot content.
